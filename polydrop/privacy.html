Polydrop Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

In short: Polydrop collects no data. It has no network access, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings and high scores stay on your Mac.

Who we are

Polydrop is a puzzle game for macOS made by David Strupl, who is responsible for this policy. Contact: dstrupl@gmail.com.

What Polydrop collects

Nothing. Polydrop doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or usage data. The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox without permission to use the network, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code from us or from third parties.

What stays on your Mac

Polydrop saves two things in its own sandbox container on your Mac:

  • your settings: the chosen setup, starting level, rotation speed and sound style;
  • the high-score tables, including the names you type for a high score.

This information never leaves your Mac and the developer can't see it. To erase it, quit Polydrop and delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.dastrupl.polydrop.

Apple

Apple handles downloads, purchases and, if you choose to share them, crash reports and app analytics under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple may give the developer aggregated, anonymous statistics; they don't identify you.

Children

Polydrop is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can still contact us with any question about privacy at the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date. A version of Polydrop that collected data would first update this policy and the App Store privacy information.
