Polydrop Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Polydrop saves your settings and high scores locally and syncs them through your Apple iCloud account when available. The developer cannot access this data. There are no ads, analytics, tracking or separate Polydrop accounts. Optional Game Center shares eligible normal-game scores and achievement progress with Apple and supports challenges through Apple Games.

Who we are

Polydrop is a puzzle game for macOS made by David Strupl, who is responsible for this policy. Contact: dstrupl@gmail.com.

What Polydrop collects

The developer does not receive your settings, player name, scores or usage data. Polydrop uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage to transmit settings and high scores between your devices signed into the same Apple account. It contains no analytics, advertising or third-party crash-reporting code. Typed high-score names and iCloud tables are not published to other players.

Polydrop also uses Apple's Game Center service to submit a score and its named-setup leaderboard identifier when a normal game ends while signed in, and achievement identifiers and progress as pieces land. Game Center associates these records with your Apple Game Center account. Named tables use pit dimensions in either orientation (for example, 3×7×12 Basic shares the Hallway table). Zero scores never submit to leaderboards. Practice, Demo and Custom score tables outside the named setups never submit scores or achievement progress. There is no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

Local storage and iCloud sync

Polydrop saves these things in its own sandbox container on your Mac:

  • your settings: the chosen setup, starting level, rotation speed, sound style and saved player name;
  • the high-score tables, including the names you type for a high score;
  • achievement progress and played setups, associated locally with your Game Center player identifier.

When iCloud is available, settings, the saved player name and high-score tables are stored in your iCloud account. Achievement progress is handled separately by Game Center. The developer has no access to that private key-value store. Polydrop works without an internet connection or an Apple account; changes remain on your Mac and sync later when available. Sync timing is controlled by Apple and is not immediate. To control iCloud access, use System Settings > your Apple account > iCloud. Switching Apple accounts returns to the start screen, saves a local backup of the previous account's progress and loads the new account's settings and scores. The old data is not uploaded to the new account.

To erase saved and synced data, return to the start screen and choose Game › Erase Saved and Synced Data… (Shift-Command-Delete), then confirm. This clears settings, the saved player name, all high scores and local previous-account backups. A reset marker syncs through iCloud; other Macs discard their older synced data when they reconnect. Previous-account backups are local only; to erase those on another Mac, use the same command on that Mac. A running game on those Macs ends without republishing its result. New games played after the reset can sync normally. This reset does not erase Game Center achievements or scores, which Apple manages separately. The reset cannot be undone. If offline, keep the app installed until the reset syncs. When your Apple account cannot be confirmed, sync pauses and no data is uploaded; the start screen explains this. A reset while sync is paused affects only this Mac. Sign in and enable iCloud Drive, wait for sync to resume, then repeat the reset on the intended account to erase its iCloud copy. Deleting ~/Library/Containers/com.dastrupl.polydrop only removes this Mac's copy, and its iCloud copy can return. We do not rely on macOS offering a per-app iCloud key-value deletion control.

Game Center and Apple Games

Apple handles Game Center account information, scores and gameplay activity under its Game Center privacy notice. Your Game Center nickname, avatar, leaderboard scores and achievements can be visible to other players. Apple's Games app can display this information on supported devices. On macOS 26 and later, optional challenges use Apple's interface to select participants and share competitions. Apple handles participation, invitations, challenge scores and Games activity; when you open a game activity, Polydrop reads only its definition identifier to select the setup. The app starts, pauses, resumes and ends the received activity without reading participant details, invitation text or other activity properties. Polydrop does not request your friends list or store your Game Center nickname or avatar. It stores your player identifier locally only to keep achievement progress separate by account. You can manage your account and sharing in System Settings > Game Center. Signing out keeps future games local; deleting local Polydrop data does not delete records Apple holds in Game Center.

Apple services

Apple handles downloads, purchases and, if you choose to share them, crash reports and app analytics under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple may give the developer aggregated, anonymous statistics; they don't identify you.

Children

Polydrop's offline play is suitable for all ages. Apple manages children's Game Center accounts and sharing restrictions through its services and Screen Time controls.

Your rights

The developer cannot access your private iCloud key-value store or local saves. You can remove names, scores and settings with the erase command described above. For help with those controls or local account backups, contact the address above; please do not send your saved data unless you choose to share it. For records held by Apple Game Center, use Apple's account and privacy controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date. A version of Polydrop that collected data would first update this policy and the App Store privacy information.
