Polydrop Support

A 3D falling-block puzzle for Mac

Questions, problems or ideas? Email dstrupl@gmail.com. Please include your Polydrop version (Polydrop › About Polydrop) and your macOS version.

How to play

You look straight down into a pit. Pieces made of cubes sink toward the floor. Move and turn them so they fill whole layers; a full layer clears. The game ends when a new piece no longer fits. Press H on the start screen, or choose Help › Polydrop Help, to see this in the app.

Keys

← → ↑ ↓Move the piece across the pit
Q W ERotate it around each axis
A S DRotate it back
SpaceDrop it to the floor
PPause or resume
OSound on or off
EscEnd the game

On the start screen, the arrow keys move between controls and change values, Return plays, and P, D, H and S open Practice, Demo, How to play and the high scores.

Questions

Do the keys work on my keyboard layout?
Yes. Keys are matched by their position, so AZERTY, QWERTZ and other layouts play the same as US English.
What are the setups?
Each setup is a pit size and a block set: Flatland, Cube Rush, Mayhem, Snack Size, Rabbit Hole, Pancake, Hallway and Big Top. Custom lets you pick a footprint from 3 to 7 cubes per side, 6 to 18 layers deep, and the Flat, Basic or Extended block set.
Why didn't my score enter the high scores?
Practice and Demo games never enter the high scores. Each pit and block set has its own table, so open the high scores with S and step to the setup you played.
How do I change or turn off the sound?
Pick Glass chimes, Soft marimba, Warm synth or Off on the start screen, or use the Game › Sound menu. Press O during a game to switch sound on or off.
How do I reset the high scores?
Return to the start screen, choose Game › Erase Saved and Synced Data… (Shift-Command-Delete), then confirm Erase Data. This clears settings, your saved player name and all high scores on this Mac and, when iCloud connects, your other Macs. Offline Macs discard games and changes from before the reset when they reconnect. This cannot be undone. Deleting the app's container resets only this Mac; its iCloud copy can return. Account changes keep a local backup of the previous account's progress; the erase command also deletes these backups on this Mac. If the start screen says iCloud sync is paused, erasing affects only this Mac: sign in, enable iCloud Drive, check whether sync resumes and repeat for the iCloud copy. If it stays paused, contact support before deleting the app's local data.
Does Polydrop need an internet connection or an account?
No. Play and local saves work offline without a Polydrop account. When available, iCloud privately syncs settings, the saved player name and high scores between your Macs using the same Apple account. The developer cannot access that data. Switching Apple accounts returns to the start screen and loads the new account's data without uploading the previous account's progress. Optional Game Center leaderboards and achievements send eligible scores and progress to Apple. On macOS 26 or later, finish a named normal game with a positive top-10 score to see Challenge a friend (C after saving your local name). It starts a fresh competition; the score just shown does not count. The button requires a challenge returned by GameKit and unrestricted multiplayer access. High scores > Challenges (C) opens existing competitions. Apple manages participants and Games activity. Play starts the matching setup at level 0 without changing your saved setup or starting level. If Play is opened during an unfinished real game, Polydrop pauses and shows a notice to finish or end it, then open Play again. Demo can be replaced directly. See the privacy policy.
Does erasing synced data reset Game Center?
No. Game Center scores and achievements are managed separately through Apple.

Requirements

macOS 15 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon or an Intel processor.
